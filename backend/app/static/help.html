Frequently Asked Questions
How do I assign a lead to another executive?
Go to the Leads page, click on the lead you want to reassign. In the top right corner of the Lead Detail modal, click the 'Assign' dropdown and select the new executive. If the lead is SLA overdue, reassignment requires manager approval.
What does the red pulse on a lead mean?
A red pulse indicates an SLA (Service Level Agreement) breach. This means the lead has not been contacted within the mandatory 5-minute window (M-03 rule). Please contact these leads immediately to stop the timer.
How do I generate a monthly performance report?
Navigate to the Reports tab. Select 'Last 30 Days' or 'This Month' from the top right dropdown. The 20-metric dashboard will update automatically. Click 'Export CSV' to download the raw data.
Why can't I see the Coaching Scores?
Coaching scores (M-09 rule) are manager-only data. Regular sales executives only see their own performance metrics. If you are a manager and cannot see this column, please contact the system administrator to check your role settings.
