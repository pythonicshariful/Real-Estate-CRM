Backblaze B2 Setup Guide

This guide explains how to connect your CRM to Backblaze B2 to securely and reliably store all your sales call recordings.

Step 1: Create a Backblaze Account

  1. Go to Backblaze B2 and sign up for a free account.
  2. Once logged in, look at the left sidebar and click on Buckets.

Step 2: Create a Public Bucket

  1. Click the Create a Bucket button.
  2. Bucket Name: Enter a unique name (e.g., mycompany-crm-recordings). It must be globally unique across all Backblaze users.
  3. Files in Bucket are: Choose Public. (This is required so the CRM can play the audio files back to you via a secure direct link).
  4. Default Encryption: Disable (or leave as default).
  5. Object Lock: Disable.
  6. Click Create Bucket.
  7. Wait for the bucket to be created. Once done, look at the bucket details and copy the Endpoint URL (it will look something like s3.us-east-005.backblazeb2.com). Add https:// to the beginning of it and paste it into the S3 Endpoint URL field in your CRM.
  8. Also, type your exact Bucket Name into the B2 Bucket Name field in the CRM.

Step 3: Generate Application Keys

  1. On the left sidebar in Backblaze, click on Application Keys.
  2. Scroll down to the bottom and click the Add a New Application Key button.
  3. Name of Key: crm-storage-key
  4. Allow access to Bucket(s): Select the bucket you just created.
  5. Type of Access: Read and Write.
  6. Click Create New Key.

Step 4: Copy the Keys to your CRM

Backblaze will now show you a blue box with your new keys. This is the ONLY time you will ever see the Application Key, so don't close the tab!

  • Copy the keyID and paste it into the B2 Key ID field in your CRM.
  • Copy the applicationKey and paste it into the B2 Application Key field in your CRM.

Step 5: Save & Test

Click Save Storage Settings in the CRM. To test it, go to a Lead's profile, click "Log Call", attach an audio recording file, and hit save. The recording should upload successfully and be playable from the activity timeline!
