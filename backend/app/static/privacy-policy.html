Southeast Landmark Ltd. is a property developer in Dhaka, Bangladesh. The App helps our sales team follow up on property enquiries, including enquiries submitted through our Facebook lead forms.

2. Information We Collect

When you submit an enquiry through one of our Facebook lead forms (Meta Lead Ads), Facebook shares with us only the information you choose to provide in that form, which may include:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number
  • Your email address
  • Your project interest (for example, residential plot or apartment) and any answers you give on the form, such as your profession or preferred area

Our sales team may also add information you provide directly to them, such as call notes, appointment times, and the outcome of a visit or conversation. The App also stores the user accounts of our own staff (name, email, and role) so that leads can be assigned and followed up.

We do not collect sensitive personal information such as passwords, payment card details, or government ID numbers through the App.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect only to:

  • Respond to your property enquiry and contact you about the project you asked about;
  • Schedule and manage site visits, calls, and follow-ups;
  • Keep a record of our conversations so that any member of our sales team can help you without asking you to repeat yourself;
  • Measure how quickly and effectively our team responds to enquiries.

We do not use your information to show you advertisements outside of responding to your enquiry, and we do not use it for any purpose unrelated to our property business.

4. Facebook and Meta Lead Ads

When you fill in one of our lead forms on Facebook, you consent on Facebook to share the details in that form with Southeast Landmark Ltd. The App receives that lead data from Meta through the Facebook Lead Ads service and stores it in our CRM so that our sales team can contact you.

The App only accesses lead data from Facebook Pages administered by Southeast Landmark Ltd. It does not access your Facebook profile, your friends list, your posts, or any other information from your Facebook account beyond what you submit in our lead form.

5. How We Share Your Information

We share your information only inside our company, with the member of our sales team assigned to your enquiry and their manager, so that your enquiry can be handled.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party, and we do not share it with other companies for their own marketing. Your data is stored on our own secure servers and is not shared with any outside data broker.

6. How Long We Keep Your Information

We keep lead information for as long as your enquiry is active and for a reasonable period afterwards so that we can answer follow-up questions and keep proper business records. When information is no longer needed, or when you ask us to delete it (see Section 8), we delete it from the App.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Ask us what personal information we hold about you;
  • Ask us to correct information that is wrong or incomplete;
  • Ask us to delete your personal information from our systems (see Section 8);
  • Ask us to stop contacting you about an enquiry.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@southeastlandmark.com.

8. Data Deletion

How to request deletion of your data:

Send an email to info@southeastlandmark.com with the subject line “Data Deletion Request”. Please include the name, phone number, and (if you provided one) email address you used when you submitted the lead form, so that we can find your record.

We will confirm receipt of your request, delete your personal information from the Southeast Landmark CRM, and reply to confirm the deletion within 30 days.

You can also remove data through Facebook’s own tools: in your Facebook Settings, go to Business Integrations, find “Southeast Landmark CRM”, and remove it; you can also delete a submitted lead from the lead form’s activity where Facebook provides that option. Please note that removing the App on Facebook does not automatically delete the copy of your enquiry already stored in our CRM — to delete that copy, please email us as described above.

9. Data Security

We protect your information with access controls: only authorised members of our sales team and managers can view lead records, and each person can only see the leads assigned to them, except for administrators who manage the team. Data is transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information from unauthorised access, loss, or misuse.

10. Children’s Privacy

Our property services and lead forms are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18. If you believe a child has submitted information to us, please contact us at info@southeastlandmark.com and we will delete it.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if the way we handle lead data changes. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page shows when it was most recently revised. The current version is always available at this page.
